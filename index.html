Single-page sites: 3–5 business days. Business sites: 1–2 weeks. E-commerce: 2–3 weeks. We can also work out a faster timeline for urgent projects.
Do you provide support after launch? +
Yes. Each plan includes free support (Starter: 1 month, Professional: 1 month, Premium: 3 months). After that, we can arrange a monthly support package.

We keep refining until you're happy. There are multiple review stages, and your feedback is applied at each step — no extra cost.

Typically 50% at project start and 50% on delivery. For larger projects, we can arrange staged payments.
