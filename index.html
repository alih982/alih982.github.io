FAQ

Any question? It's probably here

How much does a website cost?
It depends on the project. A single-page site starts from a base price, while e-commerce and business sites are higher. For an exact quote, just send me a message — I reply fast.
How long does it take?
Single-page sites: 3–5 business days. Business sites: 1–2 weeks. E-commerce: 2–3 weeks. We can also work out a faster timeline for urgent projects.
Do you provide support after launch?
Yes. One month of free support is included, and we can also arrange a monthly support package if you want.
What if I don't like the design?
We keep refining until you're happy. There are multiple review stages, and your feedback is applied at each step — no extra cost.
How does payment work?
Typically 50% at project start and 50% on delivery. For larger projects, we can arrange staged payments.
